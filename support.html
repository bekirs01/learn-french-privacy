Support for Learn French: Phrases & Words

Public support page for the Learn French: Phrases & Words iPhone app and its Apple Watch companion.

On-device name: Learn French

About the app

Learn French: Phrases & Words is an offline French vocabulary app. You can study words, phrases, and the alphabet, hear bundled French pronunciation, mark favorites, take quizzes, and optionally send a learning plan to a paired Apple Watch. No account is required.

Troubleshooting

  • Progress disappeared. Learning progress, favorites, and settings are stored only on this iPhone. Deleting the app, or restoring a device without a backup of that data, removes them. There is no cloud account to restore from.
  • Pronunciation is silent. Spoken audio uses bundled French recordings when present, otherwise Apple’s on-device French text-to-speech. Check that your device is not muted, that volume is up, and that a French voice is installed in iOS Settings if the system voice is missing.
  • Sound effects are off. Turn on Sound effects in the in-app Settings. Pronunciation is separate from that switch.
  • Apple Watch is empty. Pair the Watch with the same iPhone, install the Learn French Watch app, then send a learning plan from the iPhone Watch screen. The transfer uses Apple Watch Connectivity between your paired devices.
  • Watch reminders do not appear. Reminders are scheduled locally on the Watch after you send a plan with reminders enabled. Allow notifications for the Watch app if iOS or watchOS asks.
  • Translation language looks wrong. A fresh install defaults to English. Change Translation language in Settings. That choice stays on the device and updates learner meanings in the app.
