Privacy Policy for Learn French: Phrases & Words

This policy describes how Learn French: Phrases & Words handles information. It reflects only what this app actually does.

Effective date: October 5, 2026

App: Learn French: Phrases & Words (iPhone and Apple Watch companion)

On-device name: Learn French

Developer: Bekir Sucikaran

1. Summary

Learn French: Phrases & Words is an offline language-learning app. It does not require an account. It does not include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or social login. It does not send your learning data to our servers. We do not operate a backend for this app.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your iPhone, using on-device storage (Apple UserDefaults via the Flutter shared_preferences plugin):

  • Translation language preference
  • Favorite word and phrase IDs
  • Learned word and phrase IDs
  • Quiz miss records (content IDs, miss counts, timestamps, last quiz type)
  • Sound effects and haptics on/off
  • Apple Watch learning-plan draft (selected items, reminder window, last sync status, and last synced learner language)

This information is used only to run the app on your device. It is not transmitted to us.

On first launch, the translation language defaults to English on the device. You can choose another supported learner language. That choice stays on the device.

3. Learning content

Words, phrases, alphabet, images, translations, sound-effect files, and bundled French pronunciation recordings are packaged in the app. Core learning does not require a network connection.

4. Pronunciation and audio

Pronunciation plays bundled French recordings on this device. If a recording is missing, the app uses Apple’s on-device text-to-speech (AVSpeechSynthesizer through the flutter_tts plugin) with a French speech locale. The app does not record your voice, does not use the microphone, and does not use speech recognition.

Bundled pronunciation files and short quiz sound effects are played locally with the audioplayers plugin. The app does not stream audio from the internet and does not call a cloud text-to-speech service. The in-app Sound effects setting controls quiz feedback sounds only; pronunciation is separate from that switch.

5. Apple Watch

If you use the Learn French Watch app, the iPhone sends a learning plan to your paired Apple Watch with Apple Watch Connectivity. That plan can include French text, your chosen translation, and reminder schedule settings. The transfer stays between your iPhone and your Watch.

The iPhone may keep a pending copy of that plan in on-device storage so it can be delivered when the Watch becomes reachable.

Watch reminders are local notifications on the Watch. They do not use Apple Push Notification service and do not go through our servers.

The Watch may store the latest plan in on-device files and UserDefaults so items remain available when the iPhone is not reachable.

6. Data we do not collect

We do not collect name, email, phone number, address, payment information, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone recordings, browsing history, search history sent to us, advertising identifiers (IDFA), cookies, device identifiers for tracking, crash logs, or analytics events.

We do not use Google AdMob, Google Mobile Ads, Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Supabase, or similar third-party analytics/advertising/backend services.

7. Tracking

This app does not track you. It does not link data with third-party data for advertising. It does not use App Tracking Transparency because it does not track.

8. Third-party software in the app

The iPhone app is built with Flutter and includes these plugins, used only on the device:

  • shared_preferences (saves preferences on the device)
  • audioplayers (plays bundled sound files and pronunciation recordings)
  • flutter_tts (on-device speech synthesis)

These components are not used to send your information to their publishers. Apple provides iOS, watchOS, Watch Connectivity, UserNotifications, and speech synthesis as operating-system features.

9. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. A parent or guardian may use the app without creating an account.

10. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not receive your learning data, we do not retain it on our systems. You can remove on-device data by deleting the app (and the Watch app, if installed), subject to normal Apple platform behavior.

11. Changes

If the app’s behavior changes (for example, if a future version adds accounts, ads, or analytics), this policy will be updated and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version ships.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, email the developer at bekirsucikaran00@gmail.com, or use the support contact shown on the App Store listing for Learn French: Phrases & Words.
